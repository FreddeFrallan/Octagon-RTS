Neon Grid Rules

Welcome, Commander. You control a base on a grid of hexagons. Your goal is to destroy the enemy base.

  • Real-Time Battles: The game is played in real-time. No turns! Multiple mechs, workers, and artillery can move and attack simultaneously.
  • Bases: Your command base can build units. Build buttons appear only when you click your own base tile. Built units spawn at a random adjacent tile.
  • Workers (🪙 50): Can mine gold from adjacent resource tiles. Move a worker next to a resource tile and they will automatically gather gold (gather 25 gold every 2s).
  • Mechs (🪙 100): Combat-heavy mechs (30 HP, 8 Attack). Move mechs to attack hostiles automatically.
  • Artillery (🪙 80): Long-range shelling platforms (5 HP, 15 Attack) with a 1-2 hex range. Right-click performs default movement. Click Artillery Fire to enter Attack Mode, coloring the 1-2 range cells red, and target any hex (including enemy bases). Can shell enemy bases from adjacent tiles.
  • Movement: Select a tile containing your units. Adjacent hexes light up in green. Click or right-click a green hex to move selected units there. Units take 1.5 seconds to travel.
  • Real-Time Combat: When mechs/workers of opposing teams occupy the same tile, they automatically trade blows every 1 second. Artillery can attack from 1-2 hexes away. Destroy the enemy base to win!
